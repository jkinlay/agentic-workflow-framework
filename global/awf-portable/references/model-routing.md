Read the target's root `OPERATING_CONFIG.yaml` separately from protected governance. Default three streams fit ceiling six, one reviewer each. Direct chat changes use `workflow.py operating set`; show the table, exact refusals and verbatim instruction record. Recommendations require acceptance. Never mix evidence cohorts across operating hashes or relabel legacy unknown hashes.

Reconciliation is disabled until human-enabled operator policy. The helper records assertions; the host/operator must establish authority and termination/remediation. Preserve charges/audit and finite ticket-wide retry limits across policy changes.

Keep substantive policy cohorts separate; equivalent omitted/default representations include only narrowly matched legacy hashes without rewriting history. For external-engine review, read verified `.agentic/docs/28-EXTERNAL-REVIEW.md`; native reviewers do not qualify that gate.

Preserve unknown usage and charges; recovery must establish actual termination/authority rather than resetting accounting.
